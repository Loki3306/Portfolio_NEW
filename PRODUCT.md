# Product Definition: Premium Engineering Portfolio

## Vision
A visually stunning, modern, and interactive engineering portfolio that tells a cohesive story of technical expertise, creative development, and research depth. Inspired by the visual design languages of Linear, Apple, Stripe, OpenAI, and Anthropic.

## Target Audience
- **Recruiters**: Quick scanning for core qualifications and skills.
- **Hiring Managers**: Deep dive into actual roles, technical choices, and project impacts.
- **Engineers / Research Teams**: Evaluating coding practices, system designs, research publications, and technical depth.

## Visual Design Principles
- **Style**: Dark mode, minimal, high-contrast, clean typography, premium gradients, subtle micro-animations.
- **Typography**: Geist (sans-serif) with Inter as a fallback.
- **Layout**: Linear and Apple-inspired, grid-aligned, crisp borders, and glassmorphic elements.
- **Interaction**: Responsive, fast, with highly optimized, purposeful Three.js/R3F visualizers that run at 60 FPS without distracting the user.

## Core Sections
1. **Hero**: Clean name/role intro with an interactive 3D particle/neural network scene.
2. **About Me**: Narrative storytelling of focus and key statistics (animated counters).
3. **Skills**: Multi-row infinite horizontal marquee with official SVG icons and descriptions on hover.
4. **Experience**: Scroll-animated interactive timeline.
5. **Research & Engineering**: Animated system architecture diagrams showing EEG, OCR, and AI pipelines.
6. **Projects**: Premium cards for featured projects with Github links and demos.
7. **Achievements**: Prestigious presentation of hackathons, leadership, and competitions.
8. **Contact**: Bottom CTA with an input field redirecting to WhatsApp.
